However, I'm really lucky to run into "An Quynh" princess just when I was about to give up. I was attracted to her gentle and kind voice. I never heard so a beautiful voice before.

I'm sad that I didn't learn English well in school this year. I should have got a good level, but I was casual. I need a moment to organize my thought in English in my brain. But An Quynh is just like native, her speaking is so influence. I'm sorry.

When I asked the meaning of her name, she told the name is from grandpa. Her grandpa said the night-blooming cereus represents a delicate and elegant beauty. It booms quiety at night, with a gentle fragrance that draws people in soo. It's peaceful presence with rare and gentle beauty. I think her name is as perfect as her.

It's been always foggy in California lately. I've felt sad. I live a large house alone for a long time, cuz my grandma moved on the new farmland before a few months. I always feel longly at home. I can't deal relationship with my parents, we've lost touch in a long time, I wanna be controlled by them. An Quynh said her parents divorced when her was little. I'm really sorry when I heard that she've been through a lot. I think she was way too tough in the past. However she's always keep positive in the present. Maybe the present is the best present. Sometimes I always memory my childhood, even though it's really too tough for me. I'm just the first child in my family, and I'm actually an only child. But almost only my mother kept me company in my childhood. My mon wasn't even any patient for me. No matter what I do, if I took a mistake, I would be hit. When I grew up a little, I started to fight with her. After when I grew up too much, I almost never said anything with her, cuz she perferred believing what teacher said to trusting me. I always missed them when I was way too young, cuz I can get a lot of candies, chocolates and stuff, and they would prevent my mom from hitting me. We slowly lost touch when I came the USA about one year. I'm sad my parents almost don't care about my mod, body or something else. They only hope that I can be w ho they want me to be.
